# Quick Experience

The website we provide is a test case website for a small dinosaur jumping game. You can follow these steps to quickly experience and understand the WebSocket control function.

1. Open the DG-LAB APP. After logging in, click SOCKET on the main interface and turn on your Coyote. The app will scan and connect to the Coyote 3.0. (2.0 devices are not supported yet. Upon first use, the APP will pop up the WebSocket function introduction and precautions, please read them carefully).
2. Please open this website with a computer browser: [https://www.dungeon-lab.com/t-rex-runner/index.html](https://www.dungeon-lab.com/t-rex-runner/index.html). Domestic users can visit: [https://dungeon-lab.cn/t-rex-runner/index.html](https://dungeon-lab.cn/t-rex-runner/index.html). If you do not have a computer, you will need at least another tablet device or similar for convenience, because we need to use a keyboard to control the game in subsequent operations.
3. After opening the website, click Connect in the top right corner of the webpage, and the webpage will display a QR code.
4. Return to the DG-LAB APP, click connect to the server, select the camera icon, and aim at the QR code on the computer screen to scan it. Try to ensure the scanning box is completely aligned with the QR code. The APP will automatically establish a connection after recognising the QR code.
5. After the connection is complete, the QR code displayed on the computer webpage will disappear. At this time, you can press the space bar on the keyboard to start the game. When the small dinosaur hits an obstacle, it will trigger the waveform output for 5 seconds (this is the default setting and can be modified), and you will see your Coyote device start working.

## How to Configure

1. We have set up some pre-made options on the small dinosaur game webpage. You can click them to expand the specific menus. This operation takes effect in real time, such as output duration, output intensity settings, etc.

## FAQ

1. If you want to control multiple Coyote 3.0 devices simultaneously, you need some coding ability to modify our open-source code. Please see the README.md document for details.
2. If you need other games to support controlling the Coyote 3.0, please also check our README.md document.

# Safety Reminder

As the WebSocket service acts as a third-party control end, it is difficult for us to monitor whether its workflow is full of malicious intent, so please do not casually scan and connect to WebSocket QR codes from unknown sources.
